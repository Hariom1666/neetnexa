QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Frequently asked questions

What does a course include?

Courses are structured around NEET's core syllabus with lessons, practice questions, revision tools and topic-level progress tracking.

How do mock tests work?

Take timed tests, review your score, accuracy, question analysis and subject-wise performance, then use the insights to choose what to revise next.

Is there a free preparation option?

Yes. Start with selected study resources and planning tools for free, then explore additional course options when you are ready.

Can I make a personalised study plan?

Yes. Use the daily planner to organise tasks around your available time and upcoming revision reminders.
